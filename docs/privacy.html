Privacy Policy

Effective October 17, 2026

Textize does not collect any data. This page describes what the app does with audio and text, and what it stores on your Mac.

What Textize does not do

  • It does not collect, store or transmit the audio it captions.
  • It does not collect, store or transmit the captions or translations it produces.
  • It does not use the microphone.
  • It does not use analytics, crash reporting, advertising identifiers or accounts.
  • It does not connect to any server operated by the developer.

How audio is processed

Textize receives the audio your Mac is playing through the macOS System Audio Recording permission and passes it to the speech recognition built into macOS. When you choose a translation language, it translates the recognized sentences with the translation feature built into macOS, so both steps run on your Mac. Audio and text stay in memory only as long as they are shown and are discarded when you clear the captions or quit the app.

Downloads handled by macOS

macOS downloads speech models and translation language packs from Apple the first time a language is used. Apple's privacy policy covers those downloads. Once a language is downloaded, recognition and translation work without an internet connection.

What is stored on your Mac

Textize saves your preferences in the standard macOS preferences store on your Mac: the selected speech and translation languages, display options such as text size and background opacity, the interface language, and the position of the caption window. Caption text is not saved, and the diagnostic messages Textize writes to the macOS unified log contain only technical details such as counts and language codes.

Changes

If the way Textize handles data changes, this page will be updated and the change noted in the app's release notes.
